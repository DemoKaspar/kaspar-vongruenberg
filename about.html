About

I helped create Platform Engineering as a discipline and built several initiatives that bring it to life.

On the theory side, I write a book and essays that define the frameworks - Internal Developer Platform, Paths to Outcome, Cost of Chaos, and more recently the Agentic Platform and the four levels of agentic software development.

On the operating side:

  • Weave Intelligence: analyst firm for Platform Engineering and publisher of the book; I am its CEO
  • PlatformCon: annual conference
  • PlatformEngineering.org: open community
  • Platform University: education programs
  • Platform Engineering Consulting (PEC): training and advisory for platform teams
  • Canyon: open source control layer for coding agents
  • Humanitec: platform orchestrator

None of these are individual efforts; each exists through collaboration with partners and colleagues.

Short bio

For introductions, event pages, and anyone who needs it in the third person:

Kaspar von Grünberg is an early pioneer of the Platform Engineering discipline and CEO of Weave Intelligence, the analyst firm for Platform Engineering. He is the co-author, with Luca Galante, of Thinking in Platforms: Platform engineering as the operating model for work in the AI era (2026). He founded PlatformCon, PlatformEngineering.org and Humanitec, and has rolled out many platforms hands-on. His current work focuses on how platforms enable enterprise AI adoption. He lives north of Berlin, Germany.
