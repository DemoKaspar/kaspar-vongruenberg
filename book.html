Thinking in Platforms

Thinking in Platforms: Platform engineering as the operating model for work in the AI era, written with Luca Galante. Published 2026 by Weave Intelligence.

Available on Amazon

A multi-part book on Platform Engineering as an operating model for knowledge work.

The book attempts to separate theory from vendor practice. It focuses on first principles: what a platform is, how it structures collaboration, and how it evolves inside large organizations.

Structure

It is structured in three parts:

  • Origins: why platforms must be treated as products
  • Model: a conceptual framework ("Paths to Outcome")
  • Practice: how to build and scale a platform inside an enterprise

Approach

The aim is not to promote a specific implementation, but to clarify the underlying logic that governs all of them.

Questions and answers

What is Thinking in Platforms about?

Thinking in Platforms argues that platform engineering, a discipline born in software development, is becoming the operating model for knowledge work in the AI era. It focuses on first principles: what a platform is, how it structures collaboration, and how it evolves inside large organizations.

Who wrote Thinking in Platforms?

Kaspar von Grünberg and Luca Galante. The book was published in 2026 by Weave Intelligence.

How is the book structured?

In three parts. Origins: why platforms must be treated as products. Model: a conceptual framework called Paths to Outcome. Practice: how to build and scale a platform inside an enterprise.

Is the book tied to a specific vendor or tool?

No. The aim is not to promote a specific implementation, but to clarify the underlying logic that governs all of them.
