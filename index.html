Professional interests

I work on advancing Platform Engineering into the operating model for knowledge work in the enterprise, grounded in a software engineering perspective.

I have founded several companies and institutions in this space and rolled out many platforms hands-on.

I write independently about the theory that underpins this work in the form of a book, essays, talks, and a newsletter.

Current focus: how platforms enable enterprise AI adoption, and what an agentic platform has to look like for agents to work safely at scale.
